### 拼读练习
- c-u-te → cute (发音：kyoot)
- t-u-be → tube (发音：toob)
- h-u-ge → huge (发音：hyooj)

### 造句练习
- The baby is **cute**.
- This is a **huge** box.
- **Use** a pencil.

## 家长指导

### 教学重点
- **u_e有两种发音**：
  1. /juː/ (yoo): cute, huge, use, mute, fuse, mule
  2. /uː/ (oo): tube, rude, rule, dude, duke, tune, June, flute
- 让孩子听出区别

### 对比练习
| a_e | i_e | o_e | u_e |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| cake | bike | home | cute |
| make | like | bone | tube |
| take | time | phone | huge |
